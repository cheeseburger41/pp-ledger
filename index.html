How numbers are worked out, payouts and break-even rates

Packs: a pack's price comes out of your balance and its card's value goes back in, so a pack's PnL is the card value minus the price. A free pack costs nothing, so its whole card value counts as profit. Packs count toward Balance, Net PnL, ROI and the balance and daily charts, but not toward win rate, pick hit rate, records for entries or break-even.

Balance = starting balance + net PnL of settled entries − entry amounts of open entries. An entry's amount leaves your balance the moment you log it and only comes back, with any winnings, when it settles. A Power entry only pays when every pick hits: one miss settles it as a loss right away. Bonus lineups use promo money, so they never take from your balance and only their winnings count. When every pick hits, the payout is the entry's To Win from the app; otherwise it uses the standard multipliers below, and a push or DNP drops that pick and pays the smaller entry.

Break-even is the share of picks you need to hit for an entry to pay back its cost on average. It comes from each entry's own To Win, so goblin and demon lines are priced in; bonus lineups are left out because they cost nothing. Flex break-evens assume the lower tiers shrink in step with your To Win. Open risk counts every open entry: the best case adds each entry's To Win to your balance, and the player table shows how much payout depends on each player.

Grading: open a slip and type each player's actual stat, and the pick grades itself. You can also ask Claude in your project to grade open picks from NFL and NBA box scores. Every change is saved to ledger.json in your private GitHub repo, and the repo's history keeps each version. A payout marked est. used the standard multipliers, so check it against the app.
